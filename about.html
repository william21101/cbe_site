About Us

We're a San Francisco non-profit dedicated to sharing the fun and challenge of bridge. Through free youth mentorship, friendly games, and Saturday sessions over tacos, we help students build sharp critical thinking, make great friends, and discover a game for life.

Board Members

Meet the volunteers, mentors, and educators working together to keep youth bridge welcoming and accessible across the Bay Area.

William Zhu
President

CBE alumnus (2011 Toronto Youth NABC) who represented the USA in the 2014 and 2018 World Youth Team Championships; Google software engineer and youth game director.

Chris Chen
Treasurer

UC Davis Applied Math graduate and CBE alumnus who began playing bridge at Lowell High School and oversees CBE’s financial operations.

Deborah Murphy
Secretary

Longtime CBE board officer, retired SFUSD administrator, and organizational advisor supporting Bay Area school and youth bridge programs.

Anant Rathi
Board Member

Learned bridge with his family in Austin, Texas, began tournament duplicate in high school, and mentors youth players while editing CBE newsletters.

Andrew Sinclair
Board Member

Dan Kroll

Kendrick Chow
Board Member
