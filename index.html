Saturday Street Taco

Every Saturday, students gather in San Francisco to learn bridge, analyze hands, and play friendly games over tacos and burritos. Open to all elementary, middle, and high school students.

When Every Saturday • 1:30 PM – 4:30 PM
Where Street Taco, 16th St, San Francisco, CA
Cost 100% Free (Mentorship & Snacks Included)
